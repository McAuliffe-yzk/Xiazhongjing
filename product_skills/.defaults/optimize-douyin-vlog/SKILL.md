# 抖音 Vlog 优化

## Goal

Improve the delivery of a completed personal Vlog draft for short-video viewing while preserving the creator's own voice, facts, rhythm, and emotional restraint.

## Required Moves

1. Strengthen the first 3-8 seconds with a concrete entrance, a question, a contradiction, or an immediately meaningful event already present in the draft.
2. Make the spoken rhythm easier to record: vary sentence length, remove written stiffness, and leave natural breathing points.
3. Keep real daily details and event consequences visible; do not replace them with generic motivational language.
4. Ensure the middle advances through an event, a change of feeling, or a self-correction instead of repeating the thesis.
5. Make the ending return to an existing image, action, sentence, or posture from the draft.
6. Keep the personal style restrained, specific, slightly uncertain, and first-person. Do not turn the draft into clickbait, a lecture, a sales pitch, or an inspirational speech.
7. Preserve the Xiangzhongjing narrative arc: theme opening, montage event development, emotional climax, calm reflection, callback, and value hook.

## Boundaries

- Do not add locations, actions, dialogue, dates, people, outcomes, movie plots, sensory details, or memories that are absent from the supplied draft or materials.
- Do not rewrite the draft into a generic viral-copy template.
- Do not delete the creator's central event or insight merely to shorten the copy.
- Do not output scores, audit reports, JSON, explanations, titles, or revision notes.
- Return only the complete optimized Chinese Vlog copy.
