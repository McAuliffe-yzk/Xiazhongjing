# 素材去重表达

## Objective

Turn an extractive draft into authored prose. Keep what happened; change how the experience is narrated and understood.

## Rewrite Procedure

1. Preserve protected lines exactly and preserve every immutable fact semantically.
2. Replace copied material syntax with new sentence architecture, paragraph order, perspective, and connective reflection.
3. Expand why facts matter through supported interpretation, comparison, self-questioning, and callbacks.
4. Merge list-like facts into scenes of thought without inventing scene details.
5. Keep the creator's spoken cadence and restrained ending.

## Acceptance Standard

- No unprotected source line of 12 or more Chinese characters remains verbatim.
- Source wording overlap is low enough that the draft reads as creation, not import formatting.
- New expression carries at least half of the final wording.

## Boundaries

- Do not delete important facts merely to reduce overlap.
- Do not replace source details with imagined details.
- Do not explain the rewrite. Return only the complete revised script.
